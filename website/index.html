Why is it called Brick?+

Because sometimes things are bricked. The addon, the setup, the pull. Brick helps sort them out. Naming the solution after the problem felt about right.

Is Brick free to use?+

Yes. Brick is free and its desktop source code is available under the MIT license. Download it from the public releases page.

Who can sign in?+

Brick is built for supported World of Warcraft guilds. Sign in with a Discord account that has an eligible Raider or Officer role. If you belong to multiple supported guilds, you can switch between them in the app.

Does Brick update Advance Raid Tools?+

Yes. Brick installs and updates Advance Raid Tools, including everything that comes with the addon. Choose one or more WoW installations during setup. While Brick is running and signed in, it checks for signed updates automatically.

Does Brick support Twitch?+

Yes. Brick supports live Twitch streams and Twitch VODs, alongside YouTube. Watch your guild’s streams, then revisit the pull with player POVs, deaths, and cooldowns on the review timeline.

How does Brick use YouTube?+

Brick uses YouTube API Services to find stream details and YouTube’s player to show videos. Add a video link, or use optional channel discovery in Your streams where available. You choose what to share with each guild. Read about YouTube channel access.

Do I have to sign in to YouTube or Twitch?+

Signing in is optional in Brick. A provider may require it for some videos. Where available, Home opens the provider’s own sign-in page. Your session stays personal to you; signing in does not share a channel with your guild. See sign-in and privacy.

Who can see my guild information?+

Private guild features require verified Discord membership and roles. Profiles, presence, shared stream links, recordings, and review timing are available within the relevant guild according to those permissions. Public or unlisted videos still follow the sharing rules of their hosting platform. Read the privacy policy for the full details.

How do I request data deletion?+

Email brick@lusaggo.com with your Discord user ID and relevant guild or recording. Please never send passwords or access tokens. Removing a stream link or signing out does not automatically erase historical recordings; see your choices and rights.
